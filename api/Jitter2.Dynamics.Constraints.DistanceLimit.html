Initialize(JVector, JVector, LinearLimit)

Initializes the constraint with distance limits between anchor points.

public void Initialize(JVector anchor1, JVector anchor2, LinearLimit limit)

Parameters

anchor1 JVector

Anchor point on the first body in world space.

anchor2 JVector

Anchor point on the second body in world space.

limit LinearLimit

Allowed offsets from the initial distance between anchor points. For example, a limit of (-1, 1) allows the distance to change by at most one unit in either direction.

Remarks

Equal or reversed limits fix the distance offset at their midpoint. Default values: Softness = DefaultLinearSoftness, Bias = DefaultLinearBias.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

Thrown when anchor1 or anchor2 contains a non-finite value, or when either value in limit is NaN.
