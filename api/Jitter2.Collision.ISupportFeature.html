Extension of ISupportMappable for shapes that report the whole feature they touch with in a given direction: a point, a segment or a convex polygon.

Remarks

CollisionManifold uses the feature to place contact points. Every Shape implements it by sampling its support mapping with SupportFeatureSampler; shapes with known geometry override it, which is faster and exact. Other types implementing ISupportMappable can forward to Sample<T>(T, in JVector, float, Span<JVector>).

The feature must be the support set of the shape for all directions within tolerance (the sine of an angle) of the given direction: a face whose normal is that close to the direction is reported as the face, an edge as the edge, and a curved side as its resting point or line. Every reported vertex must lie on the shape's surface.
