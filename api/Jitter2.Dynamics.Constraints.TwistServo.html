Remarks

Uses signed joint angles in [-pi, pi], without tracking revolutions. Combine with mechanical limits that keep motion inside this angular interval.

Remarks

The target must be in [-pi, pi]. The signed angle error is not wrapped: moving from -170 to +170 degrees travels through zero. Revolution counts are not supported.

Exceptions

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

The value is not finite or is outside [-pi, pi].
