Remarks

Body 1 owns the cone frame; body 2 owns the axis being constrained. The boundaries are ellipses in swing-angle coordinates (the shortest tilt, resolved along the two frame directions). Equal lower and upper boundaries constrain the axis to the cone surface. Identical ranges in both directions reproduce a circular cone. This constraint does not restrict translation.

Remarks

The reference direction defines the first ellipse semiaxis on body 1. Its component along the cone axis is discarded. The second direction is axis cross reference direction. A positive inner boundary means the initially aligned pose lies outside the allowed region. See the two-axis overload to initialize a pose with nonzero swing.

Remarks

Each range must satisfy 0 <= From <= To < pi, with To strictly positive. The lower bounds must either both be zero (no inner boundary) or both be positive. Equal bounds in both ranges define a fixed elliptical cone surface. Fixed zero or pi tilt is unsupported; use HingeAngle with AngularLimit.Full instead.

Exceptions

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

The ranges violate the requirements above.
