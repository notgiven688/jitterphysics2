RestitutionThreshold

Minimum approach speed, in world units per second, at which a contact applies restitution. Slower impacts are resolved as fully inelastic, which keeps resting and stacked bodies calm. Lower it for scenes where slow bodies must still bounce, such as billiard balls that have nearly rolled out. CCD contacts do not apply restitution. Default is 1.
